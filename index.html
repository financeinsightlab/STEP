The Background Challenge

India Moves Forward. Can Its Footsteps Generate Power?

Heavy reliance on conventional energy sources strains infrastructure, budgets, and environmental health[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span). Yet billions of kinetic movements pass unharnessed across public transit networks daily[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).

Energy Dependence

Conventional electricity generation creates fiscal and environmental strain[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span). STEP explores decentralized harvesting to diversify urban power inputs without extra land usage[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).

Untapped Human Movement

Millions walk through Delhi Metro corridors, airport terminals, and urban walkways every day[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span). This mechanical friction and kinetic pressure remains completely uncollected[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).

Inclusive Energy Innovation

Locally captured power can support micro-tier public lighting, passenger emergency chargers, and community utilities while engaging citizens directly in clean energy participation[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
